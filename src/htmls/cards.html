Access the ARRC Right-of-way

Do you have a Blanket Permit? Yes

Submit an Access Notification Form before entering the right-of-way. Allow enough lead time for review.

Access Notification Form.pdf (opens in a new tab)

Do you have a Blanket Permit? No

Do you need temporary access only?

Complete a Temporary Access Request and wait for written approval before entry.

Temporary Access Request.pdf (opens in a new tab)

Do you need ongoing or construction access?

Apply for a new Permit. Processing times vary by project scope.

Permit Application.pdf (opens in a new tab)

Modify an Existing Permit

Does your project meet the current permit conditions? Yes

No modification is required. Continue operating under your existing permit terms.

Does your project meet the current permit conditions? No

Submit a Permit Modification Form with supporting drawings and a project description.

Permit Modification Form.pdf (opens in a new tab)

Request Temporary Access

You should discuss your needs with the security office before submitting a temporary access request. Provide dates, locations, and the purpose of entry.

Apply for a New Permit

Download the permit application package, complete all required sections, and submit supporting plans for review.

New Permit Application.pdf (opens in a new tab)

Utility Crossing Requirements

Overhead crossings

Provide clearance diagrams and coordinate with operations before scheduling work.

Underground crossings

Include bore plans, depth profiles, and utility locate documentation with your request.

Construction Safety Guidelines

Emergency Contact Procedures
