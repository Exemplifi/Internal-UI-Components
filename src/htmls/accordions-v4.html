Our Executive Team

Discover the diverse expertise of the executive leadership team steering our strategic initiatives, capital improvements, and passenger services.

William G. O'Leary is the President and Chief Executive Officer, effective November 1, 2013. He is responsible for the daily management and operations of the corporation.

Previously, O'Leary served as the Chief Operations Officer, responsible for the oversight of rail transportation, engineering, mechanical, safety, labor relations, marketing, customer service and grant administration functions. From July 2001 to March 2013, O'Leary was Vice President of Finance and Chief Finance Officer (CFO).

A Certified Public Accountant and lifelong Alaskan, O'Leary earned a bachelor's degree in accounting from the University of Alaska Fairbanks.

Michelle Maddox serves as the Chief Financial Officer.

  • Oversees financial planning and reporting
  • Leads budgeting and capital planning
  • Partners with operations on fiscal controls
  1. Review quarterly financial statements
  2. Coordinate external audit readiness
  3. Present board-level financial updates

Clark Hopp serves as the Chief Operating Officer, responsible for day-to-day rail operations and service delivery.

Andy Behrend serves as the Chief Counsel, advising leadership on legal and regulatory matters.

Jennifer Mergens serves as the Chief Human Resources Officer, leading talent strategy and workforce development.

Brian Lindamood serves as Vice President and Chief Engineer, overseeing engineering and capital programs.

Christy Terry serves as Vice President of Real Estate, managing property and development strategy.
